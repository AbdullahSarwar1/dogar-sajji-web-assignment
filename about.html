Our Story

Three generations of family tradition and authentic charcoal grilling

A Punjabi Family, A Balochi Tradition

Dogar Sajji started in 1998 as a small family kitchen, when our grandfather learned the authentic Balochi Sajji recipe and brought it home to share with everyone.

Today, three generations later, we still grill it the exact same way, slow, over open charcoal, with nothing but salt and patience.

"Real Sajji requires patience, wood embers, and pure salt, never shortcuts."

Haji Abdul Rasheed Dogar
